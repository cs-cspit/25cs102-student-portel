Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common queries regarding portal access, lab practicals, and account management.

❓ How do I log in to StudentHub?

Click on the Login link in the header, select your role (Student/Faculty/Admin), enter your CHARUSAT email and password, and click Sign In.

❓ How do I submit my lab practical assignments?

Navigate to the Assignments section, locate the relevant practical module, upload your .zip or .pdf file, and click Submit Task.

❓ What accessibility standards does StudentHub comply with?

StudentHub adheres to HTML5 semantic structural standards, WCAG 2.1 contrast ratios, explicit form label pairings, skip-to-content links, and keyboard navigation support.

❓ How can I access previous year question papers (PYQs)?

Visit the Resource Center to view and download subject-wise lecture notes and past examination papers.
