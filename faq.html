Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about student accounts, event registrations, and campus services.

1. Account Registration & Authentication

Q: Who is eligible to create a StudentHub account?

All enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students of CHARUSAT university can register using their verified university email ID (e.g., 25cs102@charusat.edu.in).

Q: What should I do if I forget my account password?

You can contact the department administrative coordinator or use the Support Contact Form to initiate a secure password reset request.

Q: How is my personal contact information protected?

StudentHub stores user credentials using industry-standard BCRYPT hashing algorithms and enforces Role-Based Access Control to ensure data privacy.

2. Campus Events & Workshops

Q: How do I register for an upcoming hackathon or workshop?

Navigate to the Campus Events Catalog, select your desired event, and click "Register for Event". You can review all your active registrations on your Student Dashboard.

Q: Are event registrations free for students?

Most university department workshops, cultural fests, and technical coding competitions are completely free for registered students unless specifically noted by the organizing committee.

Q: Will I receive an event participation certificate?

Yes. Students who successfully attend and verify their attendance through the event coordinator will receive digital certificates accessible via their dashboard.

3. Technical Support & Inquiries

Q: Can I access StudentHub from my mobile or tablet?

Yes. StudentHub is designed with responsive CSS Grid and Flexbox layouts, providing full accessibility across mobile smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.

Q: How can I suggest a new feature or report an issue?

We welcome your insights! Please visit our Feedback Page or write to us directly through the Contact Page.
