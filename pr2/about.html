About StudentHub Portal

StudentHub is a centralized academic management system engineered for seamless collaboration between students, faculty, and university administrators.

🎯 Our Mission & Purpose

To eliminate information silos across university departments by delivering an intuitive, accessible, and dynamic web interface for academic tracking and campus updates.

🌟 Core Platform Capabilities

  • Semantic HTML5 & Dynamic ES6+: Accessible to screen readers, equipped with theme persistence, modal popups, and carousel sliders.
  • Role-Based Portals: Customized dashboard views for Students, Faculty, and Administrators.
  • Real-Time Notice Dissemination: Dismissible notification banners and active campus event registrations.
