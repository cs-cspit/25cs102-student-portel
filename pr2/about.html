About StudentHub Portal

StudentHub is a centralized academic management system engineered for seamless collaboration between students, faculty, and university administrators.

🎯 Our Mission & Purpose

To eliminate information silos across university departments by delivering an intuitive, accessible, and fast web interface for academic tracking and campus updates.

🌟 Core Platform Capabilities

  • Semantic HTML5 Architecture: Fully accessible to screen readers and assistive tech.
  • Role-Based Portals: Customized dashboard views for Students, Teachers, and Admins.
  • Real-Time Notice Dissemination: Instant institutional announcements.
